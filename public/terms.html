Coaching Agreement

The terms of working together

This agreement is between you (the client) and Dalton Sprayberry, doing business as Sprayberry Fitness (the coach). It applies when you pay for any coaching plan or complete onboarding.

Version: October 9, 2026

1What you get

Online coaching only. Depending on the plan you choose, that includes a custom nutrition plan, a custom training program, or both; daily check-ins; and weekly reviews where your plan is adjusted. All coaching is delivered remotely by text, email, phone, video, and online tools. There are no in-person sessions.

Your first plan is delivered within 48 hours of receiving your completed onboarding. I reply to messages within 24 hours, Monday through Saturday.

2Payment and billing

  • Monthly plans are billed automatically every month on the same date until you cancel.
  • 3, 6, and 12-month plans are paid in full up front and cover the whole term.
  • Couples plans cover both partners on the same term.
  • Your price is locked for the length of the term you bought. If my prices change, you'll hear about it at least 30 days before your next renewal.
  • All payments are processed securely by Stripe. I never see or store your card number.

3Cancellations and refunds

  • Cancel a monthly plan anytime by texting or emailing me at least 3 days before your next billing date. You keep coaching through the end of the month you've paid for. Partial months are not refunded.
  • Full refund within 7 days of paying, as long as your custom plan hasn't been delivered yet.
  • Once your plan has been delivered, prepaid terms (3, 6, or 12 months) are non-refundable.
  • Pausing: if injury, illness, or a family emergency keeps you out, you can pause coaching for up to 2 weeks per 3 months of your term, and your end date moves back by the same amount.
  • If you stop checking in for 14 days without telling me, I'll reach out. Your term still continues during that time.

4Your part

  • Give honest, complete information about your health, injuries, and habits, and tell me promptly when anything changes.
  • Complete your daily check-ins and log your food as your plan asks.
  • Follow the plan as written, and tell me when something isn't working so I can adjust it.

Results depend on your consistency, your body, and things outside either of our control, so specific results are not guaranteed unless a written promotion you signed up under says otherwise.

5Health, safety, and waiver

I am a fitness coach, not a doctor, registered dietitian, or other licensed medical provider. Nothing I provide is medical advice or treatment, and it doesn't replace care from your physician.

  • Talk to your doctor before starting any exercise or nutrition program. If you answer "yes" to any health screen question during onboarding, you agree to get your doctor's clearance before starting training.
  • Stop exercising and seek medical help right away if you feel chest pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or sharp pain.
  • You are responsible for knowing your food allergies and medical dietary needs and for telling me about them.
  • You train on your own, at a place and with equipment you choose, without me physically present. You understand that exercise carries risks, including injury and, rarely, serious medical events, and you voluntarily accept those risks.
  • To the fullest extent the law allows, you release Dalton Sprayberry and Sprayberry Fitness from liability for any injury, illness, or loss connected to following the program, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

6Privacy and your photos

  • The information you share is used only to coach you. It is never sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Progress photos, measurements, and personal details are never posted or shared without your written permission.
  • If you agree to share a testimonial, I'll identify you only the way you approve, such as your first initial.

7The fine print

  • Either of us can end coaching at any time. If I end it for any reason other than a breach of this agreement, I'll refund any unused prepaid months.
  • If this agreement changes, you'll be notified, and the changes apply from your next renewal.
  • This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.
