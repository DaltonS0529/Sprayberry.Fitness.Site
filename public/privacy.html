Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Sprayberry Fitness ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through sprayberryfitness.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you use forms on this site — the free plan calculator, the consultation request, or the client progress tracker — we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, email address, and phone number
  • Health and fitness information you choose to share: age, weight, height, activity level, goals, injuries or limitations, and workout or body-stat entries you log in the client tracker
  • Basic usage data: pages visited and general site interaction, if analytics tools are enabled

How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your personalized meal and workout plan
  • To follow up about a free consultation or coaching inquiry
  • To operate the client progress tracker for active clients
  • To improve the site and the plans we build

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How Your Information Is Stored

Form submissions are processed through Formspree. Client tracker data is stored securely through our hosting provider, Netlify. Personalized plan requests are processed using Anthropic's Claude API to generate your meal and workout plan; your inputs are sent to Anthropic solely to generate that plan and are not used by us for any other purpose.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing sprayberry.fitness@gmail.com. If you're an active coaching client, you can also ask your coach directly to remove your tracker data.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies or browser storage to remember your session (for example, staying logged into the client tracker) or to support site analytics. These do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email sprayberry.fitness@gmail.com.
