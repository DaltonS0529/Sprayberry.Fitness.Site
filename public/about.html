About Me

More Than a Program

A little about the guy behind the plans — why I do this, and why I believe it matters more than just the numbers on a scale.

Why It Started

Why I Fell in Love With Fitness

I wasn't always the guy in these photos. I used to be heavier, angry more often than not, low on confidence, and completely lacking self-control. Fitness didn't start as a passion — it started as a way out.

It was a tough, solo road, with nobody handing me a plan or a pep talk. But it changed me. Not just my body — my temper, my confidence, my discipline, all of it.

That transformation is the whole reason I do this now. If it could change me that completely, I believe it can do the same for you.
College Athletics

I Was a College Track Athlete

I competed as a track athlete in college, and that's really where discipline stopped being a concept and became a daily habit. Showing up when you don't feel like it, trusting the training, competing against your own numbers — that mindset never left me.

Self-Coached

My First Bodybuilding Show

On June 6, I stepped on stage for my first bodybuilding show — one I self-coached from start to finish. No outside coach, no shortcuts, just me building the plan and holding myself to it.

That experience taught me the truth I now tell every client — the only real obstacle between you and where you want to be is yourself.
First Place

It Paid Off

Standing on stage was the hard part. Holding this trophy afterward was proof that a solo, self-coached effort — built on discipline instead of shortcuts — can actually get you where you're trying to go.
